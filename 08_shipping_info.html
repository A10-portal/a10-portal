Order Processing

After payment is confirmed, your order is processed within 1–3 business days. You receive an order confirmation email immediately and a shipping confirmation with tracking once dispatched.

Tracking Your Order

  • Log into your Mova99 account and go to My Orders
  • Click on your order to view status and tracking information
  • Tracking may take 24–48 hours to update after shipment

Delivery Issues

  • Not arrived after 15 business days: Contact support@mova99.com
  • Incorrect address provided: Contact us WITHIN 2 HOURS of placing the order — after that we cannot guarantee a correction
  • Marked delivered but not received: Contact support@mova99.com and we investigate
  • Damaged package: Photograph immediately and email support@mova99.com with your Order ID

Important Notes

  • Mova99 LLC is not responsible for delays caused by weather, natural disasters, or carrier issues beyond our control.
  • Multiple items in one order may ship separately.
  • Delivery estimates are in business days — weekends and holidays excluded.
  • If you entered a wrong address, contact us within 2 hours of placing the order. After 2 hours, we cannot guarantee any address changes.

Shipping Questions

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is free on every Mova99 order. There are no surprise fees added at checkout.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed in 1–3 business days, then delivered in 4–9 business days — a total of roughly 5–12 business days from the order date.

Do you ship internationally?

No. Mova99 LLC ships exclusively within the United States.

How do I track my order?

Log into your Mova99 account and open My Orders, or use the tracking link emailed to you once your order ships. Tracking can take 24–48 hours to update.

I entered the wrong address — can it be changed?

Contact support@mova99.com within 2 hours of placing the order. After 2 hours we cannot guarantee an address correction.

My order hasn't arrived — what now?

If your order has not arrived after 15 business days, contact support@mova99.com and we will investigate.
